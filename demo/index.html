The boundary of the result

The assumptions travel with the bound

Geometry & motion

  • Exact fixed 63-point mean configurations; permanent spring links.
  • Each edge weight lies in 1 ± 1/100001.
  • Stationary centered Gaussian weighted-spring Ornstein–Uhlenbeck motion.
  • Each nominal strength β lies in [B/2, B], with B ≈ 1.3986078635588654 × 10⁻¹¹.

Exposure & observation

  • Fixed frequency error from π/3 at most 10⁻¹⁴.
  • Positive rectangular exposure duration ≤ 10⁻²⁰; window-start lag ≥ 1000, both dimensionless.
  • Counts are conditionally independent Poisson observations given the full trajectory.
  • Fixed integrated normalized gain in [0.9, 1.1] and background in [0, 1].

The model bounds dependence between windows; it does not assume they are marginally independent. Maintaining the integrated signal at such short exposures may demand enormous photon rates. Position errors, drift, nonstationary preparation and non-Poisson response are outside this displayed exact-geometry certificate.

The frequency tolerance was selected after earlier bounds were inspected. This page makes no novelty, empirical-validation or optimality claim. The full analytic theorem has not been verified in a proof assistant.
